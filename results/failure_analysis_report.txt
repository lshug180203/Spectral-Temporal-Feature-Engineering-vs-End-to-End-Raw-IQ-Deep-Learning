[1] LOW-SNR COLLAPSE OF CUMULANT FEATURES (SNR = -4 dB, n = 480 bursts)
  - XGBoost accuracy: 31.67% | 1D-CNN accuracy: 48.75% (CNN - XGB = +17.08 pts)
  - Mechanism A (contraction / bias): 4th-order cumulants of independent signals add,
    and the 4th-order cumulant of Gaussian noise is 0. After RMS normalization:
        C42_obs = C42_signal * (rho / (1 + rho))^2
    At -4 dB, rho = 0.398 -> contraction factor = 0.081.
  - Mechanism B (estimator variance, finite L = 128): the sample estimate of M42 averages |r|^4;
    its variance contains 8th-order noise moments, so the standard deviation of C42_hat does NOT
    shrink with SNR while its mean contracts toward 0.
  - Measured on the test set (feature C42), |mean| / std = 'feature SNR':
      QPSK   -4 dB: mean=-0.098, std=0.154, |mu|/sigma=0.64 | +4 dB: mean=-0.436, std=0.094, |mu|/sigma=4.63 | +14 dB: mean=-0.813, std=0.056, |mu|/sigma=14.51
      16QAM  -4 dB: mean=-0.076, std=0.200, |mu|/sigma=0.38 | +4 dB: mean=-0.315, std=0.095, |mu|/sigma=3.30 | +14 dB: mean=-0.534, std=0.102, |mu|/sigma=5.24
      64QAM  -4 dB: mean=-0.110, std=0.124, |mu|/sigma=0.89 | +4 dB: mean=-0.286, std=0.099, |mu|/sigma=2.87 | +14 dB: mean=-0.506, std=0.094, |mu|/sigma=5.35
    When |mu|/sigma < 1 the class cumulant is below its own estimation noise.

[2] 16QAM vs 64QAM AMBIGUITY (SNR >= 10 dB)
  - Theoretical noise-free cumulants (1 sample/symbol): C42(16QAM) = -0.680, C42(64QAM) = -0.619 (gap 0.061).
  - Measured C42 on test set (4 samples/symbol, pulse shaped, L=128, SNR>=10 dB):
      16QAM: -0.551 +- 0.104 | 64QAM: -0.497 +- 0.103 | gap = 0.054
  - Discriminability d' = gap / pooled_std = 0.52 -> Bayes error for this single feature ~ 39.7%
  - Observed 16QAM->64QAM / 64QAM->16QAM confusion:
      XGBoost: 34.0% / 30.0%
      1D-CNN : 34.7% / 36.7%
  - Proposed remedies (NOT yet evaluated by this project): longer bursts (sigma ~ 1/sqrt(L)),
    6th-order cumulants, timing recovery to 1 sample/symbol, deeper residual 1D-CNN, more training data.

[4] STATISTICAL CAVEAT
  - Each SNR point has n = 480 test bursts; the 95% CI on an accuracy near 50% is +-4.5 pts.
    Differences smaller than the CI (typically at high SNR) are not statistically significant.
